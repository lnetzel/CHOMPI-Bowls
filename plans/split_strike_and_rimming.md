# Split keyboard into Strike and Rimming

To be able to play Tibetan bowls as realistic as possible it should be possible to do rimming of two bowls at a time. And when you do, there is less need to play strikes at the same time.

I want to split the keyboard so the lower half is made of of bowls you select (last pressed) or hold down. So if you hold down two keys, the wheel will make both sing.

Since we will split the keybed, we will lose the possibility to key the same number of bowls. BUT my idea is we start to include the black keys. Put the 12 lowest tuned bowls on the first 12 keys (black and white) (for rimming play) and put the same tuned bowls on the upper half. 

The upper half will be the same bows but will be be played as striking. They are individual bowls. But I want the same bowl pitches on both halfs. Go with the existing lower half of bowls and just reuse for the upper half, yes we will loose some of the more high pitch bowls but it's okay for now.

I think we need to increase the polyphony overall to 6.
